Players by Country

Where NHL players have come from, season by season, since 1940-41. A player counts toward a season only if they played at least 5 games that season. Use the filters to zoom into a country, draft round, team, or year range. Data: players + stats (Supabase).

📝 Notes
  • The NHL was almost entirely Canadian-born until the 1970s expansion era, when American and Swedish players started arriving in real numbers.
  • Russian and Czech/Slovak players show up sharply after 1989-90 — the fall of the Iron Curtain opened the door for Soviet and Eastern Bloc players to join the NHL.
